# ChainBreaker 0.0.1

The first public Android release of ChainBreaker.

## Daily loop

Pray -> Read -> Act -> Train -> Reflect -> Repeat

## Included

- Home daily rule of life with KJV Scripture, devotion, prayer, mission, workout, battle, and reading continuation.
- Offline KJV Bible reader with chapter navigation, search, bookmarks, highlights, private notes, reading progress, and Red Letter Mode.
- Private local battle tracking with check-ins, chain days, primary battle selection, notes, and the five-step I’m struggling intervention.
- Four-week Foundation training plan with three weekly sessions, exercise completion, duration, notes, and virtue mapping.
- Offline Brotherhood editorial library with category filtering, search, reading state, and article detail.
- My Journey growth history and delete-local-data reset action.
- Supplied ChainBreaker logo and cross-and-chain splash artwork.

## Privacy

No account, cloud backend, social feed, remote editorial loading, or network dependency is required. Personal state stays on the device. The release contains no modern licensed Bible translation or audio Bible content.

## Verification

Built with Vite, React, TypeScript, Capacitor, and the project Gradle wrapper. The signed APK was installed and exercised on a connected Samsung SM-G781W physical device, including onboarding, bottom navigation, daily completion, Bible reading/search, battle check-in/intervention, workout completion, Brotherhood article detail, and restart persistence.
